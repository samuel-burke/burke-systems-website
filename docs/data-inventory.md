## Gaps

- No privacy notice on the site, although the form collects personal data. A notice should say what the form collects, why, which vendors receive it, how long it is kept, and how to ask for deletion. It needs the owner's review (and legal advice if wanted) before publishing.
- No retention rule. Decide how long requests are kept and add a deletion routine or a documented manual step.
- Previews may write into the production table ([ci-cd.md](ci-cd.md), Cloudflare step 2).
- The visitor's name goes into the email subject line unchanged.
